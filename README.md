## How I engineer

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="25%" valign="top"><strong>01 · Product first</strong><br/><sub>Understand users, constraints, failure cases, and what success should measure before choosing a stack.</sub></td>
    <td width="25%" valign="top"><strong>02 · Clear boundaries</strong><br/><sub>Define data ownership, authorization, module contracts, and privacy boundaries explicitly.</sub></td>
    <td width="25%" valign="top"><strong>03 · Production by design</strong><br/><sub>Include CI/CD, observability, release verification, rollback paths, and operating cost in the architecture.</sub></td>
    <td width="25%" valign="top"><strong>04 · Ship and learn</strong><br/><sub>Prefer small verified delivery steps over impressive demos that are difficult to maintain.</sub></td>
  </tr>
</table>
